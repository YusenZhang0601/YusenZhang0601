# TonyRainforest

**Coastal Engineering × Scientific Computing × AI-native Research Engineering**

M.S. candidate at Tianjin University working at the intersection of computational coastal engineering, numerical simulation, GPU/HPC, and AI-native research systems.

I am interested in turning difficult technical work into workflows that are **inspectable, reproducible, evidence-driven, and scalable** — from numerical experiments and validation to multi-agent research and long-horizon engineering tasks.

## Research Focus

### Computational Coastal Engineering

- Numerical wave modeling and wave–structure interaction
- Lattice Boltzmann methods (LBM)
- Free-surface / multiphase flow and porous-media interaction
- Verification, validation, and evidence-bounded numerical research

### Scientific Computing

- GPU-accelerated numerical simulation and CUDA/HPC workflows
- Numerical diagnostics, benchmark design, and reproducible analysis
- Research tooling for simulation lifecycle, validation, and evidence management

### AI-native Research Systems

- Multi-agent research workflows and independent review
- Context, state, handoff, recovery, and long-horizon execution
- Agent evaluation, observability, and research automation
- AI-assisted literature synthesis, experiment design, code/solver work, and reproducibility

My current direction is **research-first, with AI as leverage**: use agent systems to increase the depth, reliability, and throughput of scientific and engineering work rather than treating AI tooling as an end in itself.

## How I Work

I like systems where assumptions are explicit, important state is visible, failures are preserved, and another researcher or engineer can reconstruct how a result was reached without guessing.

That usually means:

- physical questions before tool loyalty;
- direct evidence before internal narrative;
- reproducibility before polished demos;
- explicit failure boundaries before strong claims;
- automation that removes human friction rather than creating more approval work.
